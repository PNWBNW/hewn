**Each person's own pay period: the design.** Three designs, judged and combined; written up as
`docs/PAY_PERIODS.md` in the plan's third pull request.
- A pay run stays one pay period, and one payday may post several runs, oldest period first.
- Each person's next period starts the day after the last day their paychecks cover, one period of
  their own frequency. Twice a month runs 1st–15th and 16th–end; monthly is the calendar month.
- Missed periods are caught up one paycheck each, so withholding and year to date come out as if
  paid on time. At most 12 are listed; those that ended more than 31 days ago start unticked.
- A failed rail paycheck is paid only by its own Retry, from what it set aside. A new paycheck over
  its days is refused.
- Nothing new is stored, and old books and paystubs rebuild unchanged.
