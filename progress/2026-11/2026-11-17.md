## 2026-11-17 — An advisory the audit skips comes off once a fix can be installed

**What happened.** A high advisory for picomatch (only the test runner uses it; nothing the app
ships includes it) had no fixed version, so #384 let the audit skip that one id. Joshua asked how
the skip is kept from outliving its reason.

**What changed.** `scripts/check_audit_skips.py` (standard library only): for each id under
`auditConfig.ignoreGhsas` in `pnpm-workspace.yaml`, it asks the npm registry (the source pnpm audit
reads) for the advisories against the lockfile's packages and the package's published versions.
It fails when an id has no reason comment or isn't a GHSA id, when no installed package has the
advisory any more, or when a version outside the vulnerable range has been out for 7 days (the age
pnpm installs). A fix younger than that is a note, not a failure. It fails, rather than passes,
when the registry can't be read. In CI after the audit; cited under OPS-04 in
`docs/INVARIANTS.md`; T3's skip rule says so.

**Tests.** `scripts/check_audit_skips_test.py`: 13 offline tests (a fix old enough fails, a fix too
new is a note, a pre-release isn't a fix, an advisory gone, no reason, not a GHSA id, ranges with
`||`, an unreadable range fails). The live check passes today: 1 skip, no installable fix.
