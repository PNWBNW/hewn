## 2026-11-26 — The pay run checks each person's agreement before asking Shield to pay

Before a pay run asks Shield to sign any payment (Pay now, Continue, Retry, and the run after Pay
again: every press goes through the pay panel's one `start`), each person it may ask for is checked
against their agreement (`apps/business/src/chain/payee_check.ts`):

- **The address.** The business's own unspent FinalAgreement for the link's agreement id, read from
  Shield once per run from both agreement programs, must name this account as the employer and the
  link's address, exactly, as the worker. Every unspent copy for that id must agree; none found
  (or only a spent one, or only an offer) does not pass.
- **Still active.** `agreement_status` under the agreement id, in the program the record came from
  (program names through `agreement_programs.ts` and `@pnw/config`, the value through the
  generated decoder of that program), must read 1u8, ACTIVE in v4 and v5. In v4, ending an
  agreement changes only that mapping (the records are left as they were); v5 sets it on every
  pause, resume and end, together with `pair_status` under the pair tag, which always holds the
  same value. A missing entry or any other status does not pass; a read that fails, or a value that
  isn't a u8, holds the person too, saying their agreement couldn't be checked.

Anyone who doesn't pass is held for that press: the pay queue takes a `held` set (`@pnw/rail`) and
asks Shield for nobody in it, while still following a payment of theirs Shield already took and
keeping a record one of theirs set aside as theirs; everyone else is paid as before. The prompt
count leaves held people out (`release.ts`), and their line in the pay panel reads "Not paid ·
X's agreement doesn't match the address in the books" or "… is no longer active". No new screen;
nothing logged. `books/rail.ts` gained `payableRailLinks`, the links `payableLinks` pays, whole.
