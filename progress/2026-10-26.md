## 2026-10-26 — The on-chain backup's format, built (part 1 of 4)

`docs/CHAIN_BACKUP.md`, first of four pull requests: the format, pure, with no wallet and no screen.

- **`@pnw/vault` key slots.** `keySlotsOf(record)` is the data key's two wrapped copies (the
  passphrase's, and the recovery key's once there is one) and nothing of the books.
  `openKeySlots` and `openKeySlotsWithRecovery` open them into a session that writes the books into
  a vault with the very same keys. 3 tests.
- **`books/books_backup.ts`, the pieces.** A snapshot of the company document, then changes (the
  new journal entries as a plain list, other sections whole when they changed), each naming the hash
  of the state it builds on (canonical JSON, keys sorted). Entry hashes are left out and computed
  again on the way back, and the head hash must match. `rebuild` stops at a missing or out-of-place
  piece and keeps the books as of the piece before. The snapshot rule is in `wantsSnapshot`. 7 tests.
- **`chain/books_backup.ts`, the wire.** Each piece is compressed and sealed with the books' data
  key the way a receipt file is (bound to `bb-<chain>-<seq>`). A snapshot carries the key slots, and
  pieces are cut into pages that fit one letter or one page, with lookups from a finder key (username
  and address, never the passphrase). `planBackup` gives the next piece, its pages and their cost;
  `restoreBackup` finds the newest chain whose snapshot is whole and falls back to the one before
  when it isn't. 7 tests: round trips, changes, big books in pages, wrong passphrase refused,
  recovery key, another username or account finds nothing, a missing page stops cleanly, and a new
  chain after 12 changes with fallback.

**A change from the design, recorded in it:** pieces are sealed with the books' own data key, and
the snapshot carries the vault's key slots, rather than a separate content key wrapped twice. The
app never holds the recovery code after showing it once, so it couldn't wrap a new key under it;
the vault's recovery slot already does. Measured with the built code: a new business's books are one
letter (0.0066 ALEO), 100 entries one page, 500 entries two, 2,000 entries seven pages (about 0.53
ALEO). Leaving out the entry hashes took 2,000 entries from 17 pages to 7. `pnpm test` 1249/1249 (150 files).
