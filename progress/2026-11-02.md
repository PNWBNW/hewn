## 2026-11-02 — Close period, part 1: closing by date, and the period root

Joshua said go. The ledger part first, pure and tested; the screen comes next.
- **Closing by date.** `@pnw/ledger`'s `closedThrough` and `lockedThrough` are now dates, and
  `closeThrough`, `reopenFrom` and `lockThrough` take dates, so part of a month can be closed.
  Books saved with month marks read them as the month's last day when loaded (`normalizeMarks`);
  nothing already closed moves. `statusOn`, `nextDay`, `previousDay` added. The closed-date
  messages in Sales and the journal say "a closed period" instead of "a closed month".
- **The period root** (`period_root.ts`), as `docs/AUDIT_DESIGN.md` §1 set out: salted entry and
  settlement leaves (HMAC-SHA256 from one anchor secret), BLAKE3 with length-framed fields under
  `HEWN::*::v1` domains, Merkle trees whose leaf counts are in the root, `prev_root` chaining, the
  range's last entry as `chain_head`, the range as `period_id`, and inclusion proofs. Tests:
  tampering, a removed or repeated entry, an entry outside the range, a settlement pointing at
  the wrong transfer or person, a wrong secret, odd, even and empty trees, proofs for every leaf.
`pnpm test` 1359/1359 (ledger 91), typecheck clean, the app builds.

## 2026-11-02 — Close period, part 2: the Close the books tab, the anchor, the reminder

- **Transactions → Close the books** (`books/ClosePanel.tsx`): the range starts the day after the
  last close (or the books' first entry); **Through** is any day before today, suggested as the
  end of the last full month. The checklist (`books/close.ts` `closeChecks`): the range ends
  before today, nothing waiting in For review, no paycheck settling, no draft invoices; each line
  that fails says what's left and links there. Then the entry and paycheck counts, and what's
  published (one 32-byte fingerprint).
- **The chain side** (`chain/CloseShield.tsx`): the Shield check (the books' Shield account
  against the account's USDCx, as on the Dashboard) is the last line; **Close through … and
  anchor** is on only when every line passes. It closes the books (`closeRange`: the ledger mark,
  and a record with the period root and its link to the previous close; a random anchor secret is
  made at the first close and kept in the books and their backups), then signs
  `payroll_audit_log::anchor_event(root)` with a public fee (`chain/close_anchor.ts`). If the
  anchor fails, the books stay closed and the close shows **Anchor now**.
- **Closes**: each with its range, counts, and its anchor's explorer link; **Reopen from a day**
  (allowed until locked; undone closes are kept, marked reopened; the next close chains to the
  last one standing; an unchanged re-close reuses the earlier anchor, since the chain refuses a
  hash twice) and **Lock through …** (for good).
- **Dashboard**: from the 5th, "Time to close October" with a link, until it's closed.
- Not yet: the period proof export (#224 step 28), next.
