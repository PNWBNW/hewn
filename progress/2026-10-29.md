## 2026-10-29 — Proof of income, the design

Joshua asked what new facets Hewn could add, and picked proof of income: a worker proves a
statement about their pay ("at least $3,000 every month for 6 months") to a landlord or lender
without showing a wage. `docs/PROOF_OF_INCOME.md`: monthly earnings statements, private records
only an employer with an agreement can issue (the check `hewn_credentials_v1` makes), revoked and
reissued to correct; a `prove` transition the worker signs, which records only a claim commitment
bound to the verifier's code; a public verify page that needs no account and keeps the claim in
the link's fragment. Why not the USDCx records, the paystub letters or `paystub_receipts.aleo`
(anyone can mint those). Limits said plainly (a fake employer, statements not tied to the USDCx,
fishing), and eight questions for Joshua. New Leo when built (Fable 5.1, INV-22). Docs only.

## 2026-10-29 — Proof of income, reworked around paystub records

Joshua: the paystubs, straight from the business, should be the source of truth, and the worker
should prove from them exactly what a third party needs (his example: earning $7,500 a month,
proving "at least 3× a $1,200 rent"). `docs/PROOF_OF_INCOME.md` now makes the paystub itself a
private record from a new program, `hewn_paystubs_v1`, issued in place of today's paystub letter
(the same one approval): the provable numbers as fields, the rest sealed under the pair's key, and
the books' paystub hash. Issuing writes nothing public (no headcount at a block); only a void
leaves a commitment. The worker's `prove` chooses claim, threshold, gross or net, period and
extras; monthly summaries for long periods; a year-to-date claim from one paystub. Monthly
statements are dropped. Nine questions for Joshua. Docs only.
