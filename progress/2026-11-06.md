## 2026-11-06 — Veil: the three questions for Provable, answered from the source

Joshua asked for a deep dive before writing to Michael. Recorded in `docs/VEIL.md`:

- **Which Leo writes the ABI codegen reads:** every Leo from 4.0.0 (`leo build`, and `leo abi`
  from deployed bytecode). Tried in the scratchpad: `leo abi` 4.0.0 on the live `hewn_letters_v2`,
  then `veil-codegen` 0.15.0, gave typed letter, page and send bindings. Leo changed the ABI's
  shape after 4.0; Veil's parser reads both (read from both codebases; running Leo 4.4.6's binary
  here was refused, so its output waits for CI's pin bump). Step 2 is unblocked.
- **`simulate` through Shield:** no. Veil's `simulateContract` throws for wallet accounts.
- **The freeze-list proof in the wallet:** the standard defines it as a derived input and Hewn's
  adapter carries the types, but adapter 1.3.0 doesn't ask the wallet what it supports (1.5.0
  does). Whether Shield computes it is the one question left for Michael.
- Also: Shield adapter 1.3.0 already has `transitionViewKeys`, so the period proof's fourth check
  needs no Veil.
- **Leo and the protocol moved** to `aleo-labs-protocol` (Joshua spotted Leo 4.4.6 there); Leo
  releases are `leo-lang-v…` since 4.1. I had first reported 4.0.2 as the latest, from the old
  tag names; corrected, with a lesson. Joshua forked Leo to `PNWBNW/leo`; the Veil fork's three
  rules apply to it. `docs/ALEO_CONSTRAINTS.md` gains a dated note and the new release links.
