## 2026-10-30 — Proof of income: an income letter, no verifier's code

Joshua: a verifier handing out a code is clunky, and landlords may not be comfortable with
technology; it should feel like bringing in paystub copies, showing only what's needed while still
proving the income came from a legitimate source. `docs/PROOF_OF_INCOME.md` now has the worker
pick a preset (rental: 3× rent, loan, proof of employment), sign once, and get a one-page income
letter with a QR code. The verifier scans it with a phone camera (no app, account or wallet), sees
a green check, and compares the name to the applicant's ID. The name is the employer's word: each
paystub record carries a salted fingerprint of the worker's legal name from payroll, and `prove`
checks it on every paystub used, which replaces the verifier's code and the live signature check.
The legitimate-source section (only the employer can issue; the employer named, with its time on
Hewn; maybe a licensed-employer badge) and lookalike sites added; questions now eight. Docs only.
